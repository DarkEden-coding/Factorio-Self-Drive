# Student policies for the Lua mod: results

Can a network small enough to run inside a Factorio mod in Lua drive about as well as the
2.1M-parameter teacher (`python/runs/ft-strong/best/model.pt`, reactive, `spec_hash`
`16a3d4353d1658c1`, about 22M multiply-adds per decision)? On the simulator holdout maps, yes:
every student, down to a 2.6k-multiply-add MLP, reaches about the teacher's success and SPL in all
five scenario categories, and all beat the A* baseline. The open risks are elsewhere: how closely
the in-game route guidance matches the simulator's, and the real Lua throughput (see "Limits").

Recommendation: ship **mlp64 + PPO** (`python/runs/students/mlp64-rl.json`). It matches or
beats the teacher in every category, has the fewest contacts of the cheap students, costs
13,312 multiply-adds per decision (under half the 30k budget), and its export is 139 KB. If Lua
turns out slower than assumed, **mlp32 + PPO** (5,632 multiply-adds, 62 KB) is the fallback at the
same success rates, with somewhat more contacts.

## Setup

- Student observation: 135 features (docs/rl.md, "Student observation"): 32 rays over the tile
  raster (clearance to the first hard, tree and nest-zone tile, range 32 tiles beyond the hull),
  4 points along the route (4, 8, 16, 32 tiles along the distance field's descent path) and the
  remaining route length, plus 30 vehicle and target features shared with the teacher. Rust cost
  is 9.0 us per observation against 47.1 us for the teacher's grids.
- Students: MLP actors [16,16], [32,32], [64,64], [128,64], and `conv` (circular 1D convs over
  the rays, 3x32 -> 8x32 -> 8x16, then a [64,64] MLP). Only the actor ships. The critic is a
  separate [256,256] MLP used only in training.
- Distillation (`fsim-rl distill`, configs `python/configs/student_*.toml`): DAgger with 256 envs
  x 64 steps per iteration. Beta (the chance of executing the teacher's action) falls from 1 to 0
  over 60 iterations. Every visited state is labelled with the teacher's log-probs and value.
  Loss: KL(teacher || student) + 0.5 x value MSE over a 1M-sample aggregated buffer. It uses the
  finetune_strong scenario mix and rewards, with standard episodes at the teacher's final
  curriculum stage. Each run stopped when the fresh-rollout KL had not improved 2% in 40
  iterations: 246-266 iterations, about 13 minutes each with four in parallel on the RTX 5060
  box. Final fresh KL: mlp16 0.073, mlp32 0.060, mlp64 0.053, mlp128 0.049, conv 0.048.
- PPO fine-tune (`fsim-rl train --init-from`): finetune_strong rewards and scenarios, 256 envs x
  128 steps, lr 2e-4, no KL-to-teacher term (`ppo.teacher_kl_coef = 0`; the option exists).
  Budget 15M steps (457 iterations). The runs were checked for a plateau and stopped early:
  between the last 30 and previous 30 iterations, training-map return, success, SPL and
  per-scenario success changed by less than 2 standard errors, and value loss was flat, for all
  five runs (evidence in `python/runs/students/logs/early-stop.log`). They were stopped right
  after a checkpoint save:
  - mlp32, mlp128 and conv at iteration 310 (10.2M steps)
  - mlp64 at iteration 340 (11.1M steps)
  - mlp16 ran the full 457, because it was nearly done after resuming.

  Wall time was 21-24 minutes per run with five sharing the 16 CPU threads. mlp16 ran its first
  220 iterations on the Mac, then resumed on the Linux box.
- Evaluation: `fsim-rl suite`, holdout split, 200 episodes per category, seed 12345, 16 envs,
  deterministic (argmax) actions, on the Linux box (CUDA). Every row uses the same episodes. The
  teacher and A* rows were re-run with the current code (`teacher-suite.json`). They differ
  slightly from `ft-strong/best/final-suite.json`, e.g. standard 1.000/0.977 vs 0.995/0.972,
  most likely because that table came from the Mac (MPS). With 200 episodes, one episode is 0.5
  points of success; differences of 1-1.5 points between students are within noise.
- Lua estimate: assumes 20M simple Lua operations per second (one arithmetic op with its table
  reads). It counts 2 per multiply-add and 1 per normalized input or ReLU. Ray casting adds 8 per
  tile visited, over 1,384 tiles: the worst case (no hits), a bound for all students. One decision
  runs every 4 ticks (66.7 ms of game time). Not measured in Factorio.

Raw results: `python/runs/students/` (`*-suite.json`; `*-export.json` for cost; `<name>-<stage>.json`
for the exported weights; `report.md` is regenerated by `scripts/student-report.py`).

## How much the route features carry the result

The route features come from our distance field: its steepest-descent path, dilated by the hull,
with extra cost near nest zones and trees. In the game they would come from Factorio's
pathfinder (`LuaSurface.request_path`) or a Lua Dijkstra. To see how much the students rely on
them, the distilled mlp64 was re-run on the same holdout episodes with the route altered
(`scripts/student-ablation.py`, `python/runs/students/mlp64-distill-ablation.json`):

- **straight**: no pathfinder at all. Every route point lies on the straight line to the target,
  and the route length equals the straight distance.
- **zone-blind**: the route comes from a field with `nest_zone_cost = 0`. This is closer to what
  Factorio's pathfinder gives, since it knows nothing about nest zones (hull dilation stays on).

| category | normal: success / SPL / zone time | straight: success / SPL / zone time | zone-blind: success / SPL / zone time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| standard | 1.000 / 0.981 / 0.000 | 0.950 / 0.931 / 0.000 | 1.000 / 0.981 / 0.011 |
| long | 1.000 / 0.998 / 0.000 | **0.425 / 0.412** / 0.022 | 0.985 / 0.983 / 0.020 |
| forest | 1.000 / 0.977 / 0.000 | 0.975 / 0.955 / 0.000 | 1.000 / 0.978 / 0.000 |
| reverse | 0.980 / 0.935 / 0.011 | 0.950 / 0.915 / 0.013 | 0.985 / 0.939 / 0.055 |
| nest | 0.995 / 0.988 / 0.004 | **0.800 / 0.798 / 0.498** | 0.985 / 0.977 / **0.402** |

Tree contacts per episode under straight: long 85.9, forest 24.0 (normally 2.8 and 5.0).

Reading:

- Over short distances the rays do the driving. Without any route, standard, forest and reverse
  lose only 3-5 points of success. Long routes depend on the route: success falls from 1.000 to
  0.425, because the 32-tile rays can't see around large obstacles, while the teacher had a
  256-tile coarse grid.
- Nest-zone avoidance is entirely the route's doing. With a zone-blind route the student still
  reaches the target (0.985) but spends 40% of the episode inside nest zones (normally 0.4%), and
  the straight route is the same (50%). The `zone_*` rays are in the input, but the distilled
  student hasn't learned to steer around zones from them, because the teacher's field-based
  route always did it.
- So in game, success should hold with any reasonable pathfinder. Zone avoidance holds only if
  the mod's route avoids zones. That means a Lua Dijkstra or A* with a zone penalty, or
  `request_path` with the zones blocked out, plus hull clearance. Plain `request_path` waypoints
  would make the car drive through nest zones. The zone-blind result is a sim proxy, not an
  in-game measurement; the game pathfinder's waypoint spacing and corner cutting also differ.

## Stopping at the target (mlp64-stop)

mlp64 + PPO treats the target as reached as soon as its center enters the 3-tile radius, so it
drives through at full speed (60-87 km/h at the end of an episode). `mlp64-stop` is the same
actor fine-tuned with `arrival.mode = "stop"` (docs/rl.md, "Episodes"). Success now means
stopped (|speed| <= 0.01 tiles/tick) within 1 tile of the target point. The fine-tune also uses
the `arrival` and `target_speed` reward terms and the `arrival` scenario (short targets, some
approached at speed or backward). Observation layout and both spec hashes are unchanged.

- Config `python/configs/student_mlp64_stop.toml`: half the episodes `arrival`, half the old
  mix, 256 envs x 128 steps, lr 1e-4, CUDA, initialized from
  `runs/students/mlp64-rl/checkpoints/latest.pt`. Budget 8M steps.
- Training took about 4.5 minutes on the RTX 5060 box at 15-35k steps/s. Training-map success
  went 0.45 (iterations 1-20), 0.74, 0.95, 0.98, 0.996 (81-100), 0.998, 0.999 (121-133). Mean
  speed at episode end fell from 18.7 to 0.2 km/h. Success had been flat since iteration 81, so
  the run was stopped right after the iteration 140 save (4.59M steps;
  `python/runs/students/logs/early-stop.log`).
- Files: `python/runs/students/mlp64-stop/` (checkpoint, `mlp64-stop.json` export, suites,
  metrics, training log).

- Under the stop rule the old student mostly fails: it passes the target and times out or stalls

## Limits and what is not verified

- Route guidance, as above: the mod needs a zone-aware pathfinder with hull clearance, and
  enough route (32 tiles of lookahead plus total length) on long trips.
- Sim-to-game gap of the inputs: rays read a 1-tile raster of entity boxes. The mod must
  rasterize the same way (docs/rl.md has the exact definitions). Anything the simulator lacks
  (player structures, biters) is outside the training distribution.
- The Lua cost is an estimate (20M simple ops/s assumed). Using that rate, mlp64 needs about 27k
  ops (1.4 ms) for the network and up to 11k (0.55 ms) for worst-case rays per decision, once
  every 4 ticks, and can be spread over those ticks. Not measured in Factorio, and the
  `student_ref.py` loop structure is not the Lua port itself.
- Contacts: the students, especially mlp16 and mlp32, still touch rocks and trees more than mlp64
  in some categories. Every contact costs vehicle health in game.
- Statistics: 200 episodes per category. Students differ from each other and from the teacher by
  about 1-1.5 success points at most, which is within noise. The contact counts and the route
  ablation are clear differences.
- PPO runs were stopped at a plateau (iterations 310-340 of 457, except mlp16). Training-map
  metrics were flat; a longer run might still cut contacts a little.
