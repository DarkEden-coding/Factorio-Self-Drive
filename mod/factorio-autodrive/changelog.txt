Version: 0.9.0
Date: 2026-11-09
  Features:
    - Drive on Vulcanus, Gleba and Fulgora. Lava and deep lakes block the way, wetland and oil
      ocean are slow ground, and demolisher territory is avoided like nest surroundings.
    - Routes weigh slow ground by travel time.
    - Retrained driving model covering the three planets as well as Nauvis, trained also on
      long trips, near-collisions and getting unstuck. It keeps more room around rocks, cliffs
      and trees and hits them much less often.
    - Routes keep a margin around obstacles instead of hugging them.
    - Cars on Vulcanus coast instead of accelerating above 130 km/h, where its open plains
      otherwise let fast fuel outrun what the model can see.
    - A vehicle pushing against a cliff or rock without moving tries driving straight, then
      backing off in each direction, instead of repeating the blocked maneuver.
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed a script error when a route reached ungenerated terrain.
    - Fixed trips stalling at a destination beside a tree, plant or rock: guidance no longer
      loops back through an earlier route section, and arrival is detected there.
    - Fixed trips through cliff mazes ending as "outside-route-region": a route that runs along
      the edge of its planning area is planned again with a larger area.
    - Fixed winding trips timing out: the time limit now follows the route length estimate,
      not the straight-line distance.

Version: 0.8.0
Date: 2026-11-06
  Features:
    - Vehicles wait before a level crossing when a train could reach it before they are across. Automatic trains are predicted from their path, manual trains from the track ahead.
    - Parked trains are obstacles in route planning. The route is updated when a train parks or leaves.
    - Trains near the vehicle appear as obstacles in the driving model's input.
    - New per-player setting "Avoid train tracks": routes go around ground-level tracks where a nearby detour exists, and cross them only when needed.
  Changes:
    - Waiting for a train does not count toward the trip's timeout or stall limit.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.7.2
Date: 2026-11-04
  Changes:
    - Movement keys only take over in the normal world view, not in map or remote view.
    - Player buildings that block vehicles are now hard obstacles in the driving model's input and route planning.
    - Any other entity that blocks vehicles, from any mod and of any type (including non-rock simple entities), is a hard obstacle too.
    - Buildings that other mods place or remove without an event are noticed near the vehicle within a second, so the car drives around them.
    - Rotating, flipping or cloning a building, and placing or removing landfill, update the obstacle map during a drive.
